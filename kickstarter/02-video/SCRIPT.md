“Settings is a page too. And Voice Mode with the Neuriy AI face lets you talk naturally — still Ello5 underneath.”

“We’re live on Kickstarter to fund always-on learning, Marketplace for builders, and public beta access. Back Neuriy — and own the assistant that keeps getting better.”

Neuriy AI · ElloFive · Marketplace · IDHook

- 1280×720 or 1920×1080, 24–30 fps
